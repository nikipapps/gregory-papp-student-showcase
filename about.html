A little background

About me

More than a list of skills, this page shares the interests, learning habits, and ambitions behind my work.

Hello, I'm Gregory.

I'm building my knowledge in web design and information technology. I enjoy discovering how websites are structured, how design choices affect the experience, and how code can turn a concept into something people can interact with.

I'm approaching learning as a process: understand the basics, practice them, test the result, and improve what isn't working. Debugging can take patience, but each problem I solve helps me become more confident and independent.

What I enjoy

Raising geckos

Animal care has taught me to be observant and consistent. Learning about a gecko's habitat and behavior encourages me to pay attention, research carefully, and take responsibility.

Video games

I enjoy games for their creativity, storytelling, and challenges. I like how they can reward planning, experimentation, and persistence—qualities that are useful when learning technology too.

Exploring technology

I'm interested in the systems and software people use every day. Learning HTML and CSS is an early step toward understanding how digital experiences are designed and built.

How I approach learning

  • Break complex assignments into smaller, manageable tasks.
  • Practice concepts by building real pages and components.
  • Test the result on different screen sizes and fix issues.
  • Ask questions, research solutions, and learn from mistakes.
  • Keep improving instead of expecting everything to be perfect on the first try.
